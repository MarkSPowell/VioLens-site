Open SAZ files in your browser

Look inside a Fiddler .saz session archive, or convert it to HAR, without installing anything. This page reads the file on your computer. It isn't uploaded anywhere.

Drop a .saz file anywhere on this page, or

#ResultMethodHostURLBodyContent-Type

Click a session, or use the arrow keys, to see its headers and body.

Need more than a quick look?

The VioLens app opens the same files and lets you search inside bodies, compare two sessions side by side, replay and edit requests, and capture new traffic. It's an open source alternative to Fiddler Classic, free for any use, for Windows, Mac and Linux.
